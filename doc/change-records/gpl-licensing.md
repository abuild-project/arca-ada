# GPL licensing and documentation policy

Arca uses GPL-2.0-or-later throughout its maintained project-owned files.
The CPIO, TAR and stream-copy units derived from Abuild retain their original
GPL licence and copyright attribution. A single licensing policy matches the
library's GPL use and removes the need to maintain an additional LGPL grant
and a separate original-source publication condition.

LICENSE contains the unmodified GPL version 2 text. COPYRIGHT, SPDX headers
and contribution guidance use GPL-2.0-or-later. The licensing guide records
the imported units, exact source revision and stream-copy subset, the GNU
ustar fixture's origin and third-party terms. Installed licence and copyright
notices retain their byte-for-byte checks. Distribution of binaries requires
the corresponding source and applicable build/install inputs.

The initial predecessor comparison is retained as historical extraction
evidence. Its optional utilities, comparison cases and command-line switches
are removed together with the separate grant inventory. Ongoing validation
uses the existing independent decoders, external readers, reference fixture,
unit contracts and Abuild integration. Source inventories and original-source
snapshots are no longer prerequisites for publishing Arca.

The reviewed agent guide applies independently of agent vendor or interface.
New maintained documentation explains behavior, reasons, compatibility and
observed evidence without session history, ticket bookkeeping or delivery
status in change-record bodies. Historical records and dated decisions keep
their original claims; references to removed files identify their immutable
historical revision instead of linking to a missing current path.

The recorded repository-protection policy and completed local Abuild
integration statement are retained. This change does not configure GitHub,
change repository visibility or modify CLI behavior. Runtime Ada/C code,
GPR build/install settings and byte-sensitive fixture inputs are unchanged;
source edits there are limited to licence and attribution comments.
